Three ways to present an independent hatmaker.

Each study is a working homepage with its own typography, colour, image treatment, navigation, motion and commerce path. Every photograph is a labelled placeholder. Product details come from the public shop and link back to it.

  2. BThe ObjectThe hat first. The felt is the colour system; choosing a felt re-colours the page and changes the hat. Anatomy as graphic language. Archivo.Open →
  3. CThe Living ArchiveThe workshop first. A filterable archive of hats, felts, bands, charms, process, people and open questions, each with an accession number. Newsreader and DM Mono.Open →
  4. WWordmark studiesSix typographic directions tested on screen, woven label, lining stamp and business card.Open →
