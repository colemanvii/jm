Design decisions

Continuity with Study B
  1. 01One typeface, three voicesArchivo alone, as in Study B: the wide, tracked wordmark; the condensed display face for names and statements; a quiet regular for text. No second family was added, including on the Driving Club page.
  2. 02The felt is the paletteWhiskey, natural, granite and pecan come from the hats themselves, with ivory and near-black. Each page opens on one field: whiskey for the house and the product, paper for the collection, black for the maker and the commission.
  3. 03Study B’s hero, kept intactThe product page keeps Study B’s drawing, annotations, swatches and oversized name unchanged, and adds what a real product page needs below it: specification, details to photograph, how to order, the other felts.
  4. 04Technical drawing as the signatureThe annotated drawing describes the hat on the home and product pages, and the jacket on the Driving Club page. It is used where it explains something, not as decoration.
  5. 05The Driving Club’s personality comes from its drawingIts paper and red ink are sampled from the original Cowboy George drawing; colour is otherwise confined to the patches, which are embroidered objects. Same header, grid, type and footer as the rest of the house, so it reads as part of Joseph’s world, not a microsite.
  6. 06Honest gapsWhere a photograph or Joseph’s own words belong, the page shows a quiet, labelled frame with the brief. Nothing synthetic or stock stands in.
  7. 07Four menu itemsHats, The Hatmaker, Driving Club, Commission. Study B’s “Anatomy” lives inside Hats. On phones the menu opens as a full-width list in the page’s own field.
  8. 08Motion only where it explainsThe line drawing draws itself once; felts cross-fade; the collection columns open on hover. All motion is off under reduced-motion settings. No scroll effects.

Still required

Before this can be real
  • Product photographs: each hat in profile on a matched ground (O1), so the annotations can sit on the real hat; three-quarter, crown-down and detail macros (O2–O4).
  • Portrait of Joseph at the bench (M1), plus a close portrait (M2).
  • Shop photographs: steam, crown, brim, stitching, the last decision (H1–H5).
  • Calibrated felt swatches with a grey card (T1), to correct the on-screen colours.
  • Joseph’s biography in his words (150–250 words), one line per hat, care notes, a commission FAQ.
  • The current logo and label files, and who made them.
  • Music City Driving Club drawings: scans of the November 20, 2024 sheet; the winged wheel, crossed flags and GO as separate line art. The page switches them on from assets/mcdc/manifest.json.
  • Jacket photographs: the flat from above, then worn; and any sample, if one is made.
  • Confirmations: AHAB’s price and category, ORIS’s availability, WOODROW, CECH and RALPH details, one turnaround per kind of hat, how commissions join the waitlist, the handover step.
  • Permission to publish the Jackson and Oxford history, and the address on a new site.
