# Content requirements and interview questions

Nothing in the studies puts words in Joseph's mouth. Wherever his voice belongs, the studies show a labelled gap. This file lists what's needed to fill those gaps.

## A. Facts to confirm with Joseph before launch

1. **AHAB price**: $1,675 or $1,395?
2. **Turnaround**: one statement each for ready-to-wear, made to order and custom (currently 6–8 weeks, 4–6 weeks and "immediately" in different places).
3. **The commission process**, step by step. The studies show Conversation → Fit → Making → Handover. Only the first step (in person or remote) is confirmed.
4. Is the second ARTEMIS listing (/products/aurora) intentional?
5. Which sizes are offered, and how do fittings work for remote customers?
6. Are custom straw hats offered year-round?
7. Which felts and materials does he use regularly (for the colour system and the archive)?
8. Whether the investment-banking background mentioned in a podcast listing may be published, and in what words.
9. Wordmark: who made the current mark, are original files available, and how attached is he to it?
10. Shopify: plan, theme, apps in use, and who has admin access (needed for any integration; **not** to be changed without approval).

## B. Interview questions (for the Maker story and the archive)

*Recorded conversation, about 60 minutes. Quotes used only with his approval of the final wording.*

**Origins**
1. What is the first hat you remember noticing on someone else?
2. You've titled your story "Fourth Time's a Charm". What were the first three?
3. Why Nashville?

**The work**
4. Walk me through a hat from the felt body to the box. Which step do you enjoy most, and which is hardest?
5. What does your stitching mean, and when did you start doing it?
6. What makes a hat fit well, beyond the size number?
7. Which decision on a hat do most people never notice?
8. How do you choose a charm or adornment? Do customers bring their own meanings?
9. Dress weight or western weight: how do you decide?

**Commissions**
10. What happens in the first conversation with a customer?
11. Has a customer ever changed how you make hats?
12. What will you turn down?

**Point of view**
13. What is a hat *for* in 2026?
14. Whose work, in any field, do you admire?
15. What would you like someone to feel the first time they put on one of your hats?

## D. Voice

Confident, understated, specific, warm. Short sentences. Concrete nouns: felt, steam, brim, thread. Never: "timeless", "elevated", "crafted to perfection", "where tradition meets innovation". If a sentence could describe any luxury brand, rewrite it.
