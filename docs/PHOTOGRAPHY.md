# Photography requirements and shot list

Photography carries this site. Until it exists, every image in the studies is a **labelled placeholder**: a tonal frame with a written brief. Stock photography is never used to stand in for Joseph, his shop, his customers or his hats.

## Standards for every shot

- **Real and authorised.** Joseph's shop, Joseph's hands, Joseph's hats, real customers with signed releases.
- **One light where possible.** Window or a single hard source; keep the shadows.
- **No props, no styling clichés.** No horseshoes, saddles, whiskey glasses or staged "workshop" clutter. Don't tidy the bench either.
- **Colour fidelity.** Shoot a grey card with each felt so the site's felt colours (currently estimates) can be matched exactly.
- **Delivery:** RAW plus 16-bit TIFF; long edge ≥ 5000 px; crops supplied for 4:5, 3:2 and 1:1. The site generates responsive AVIF/WebP from these.
