How a commission works

* Steps to confirm with Joseph
  1. 01

    Conversation

    In the shop on Villa Place, or remotely. What the hat is for, how it should sit, what it should carry.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Your head is measured, at the shop or at home with the sizing guide. The hat is made from those measurements.

    Sizing guide ↗
  3. 03

    Making

    Made by hand, start to finish, in Nashville. About four to six weeks for custom fur felt.

  4. 04

    Handover*

    The finished hat, shipped or collected.

    How handover works, and whether there is a final fitting, is to be confirmed.
