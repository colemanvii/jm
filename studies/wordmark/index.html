Wordmark studies — Joseph Martin

Six typographic directions, each tested at the four places a hatmaker’s name has to live: the screen, a woven label, a lining stamp and a business card. These are explorations, not a replacement. The current Joseph Martin wordmark has not yet been studied (the live site was unreachable from this session) and must be compared side by side before anything is chosen. All studies

Early read

01 and 06 together are the most complete system: a title-page name for the screen and print, a monogram for the hat itself. 03 is the most legible woven at small sizes. 02 is the most personal.

What a decision needs

The current wordmark beside these. A woven-label proof from the actual mill (thread count limits detail). A hot-stamp test on real lining material. Joseph’s own preference.
